About this model

This site projects college football games and compares its numbers to the current Vegas lines.

How it works

  • Every team gets an offense and a defense rating built from past game scores.
  • Each game is simulated 20,000 times to produce a projected spread, total, score, and win chance.
  • Early in the season the model leans heavily on the Vegas lines and gradually trusts its own numbers more as results come in.
  • Best bets are limited to spreads and totals (and moneylines priced near even money), ranked by expected return per 1 unit risked, with a cap on how many appear each week.
  • The units tracker shows what 1 unit on every best bet would have returned, assuming -110 prices.

Data

Odds come from The Odds API. Schedules, results, rankings, and logos come from CollegeFootballData.com. Odds refresh about every 6 hours.

Please note

This is a hobby project for information and entertainment, not betting advice. The model settings are untested estimates and past results do not predict future results. If gambling is a problem for you, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
